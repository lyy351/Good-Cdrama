The Story 剧情简介

An unexpected encounter awakens a mysterious woman and draws a man into the secrets surrounding her past.

Why I Recommend It 我为什么推荐

I love how Yu Yu centers women in her stories, and I love the fantasy flavor drawn from Chinese folklore.

我喜欢尾鱼故事里以女主为叙事中心的方式，也喜欢这种融合中国民俗想象的奇幻气质。
