The Awakening Age

觉醒年代
Aired播出2021
Genre类别Historical · Political · Ensemble
Douban豆瓣评分9.3 / 10China's most influential film & TV rating community中国最具影响力的影视评分社区之一
Episodes集数43
Director导演Zhang Yongxin张永新
The Story 剧情简介

Set between 1915 and 1921, the series follows Chen Duxiu, Li Dazhao, Hu Shi, Lu Xun, and others during the New Culture Movement — a pivotal period in modern Chinese intellectual history.

故事发生在 1915 至 1921 年之间，聚焦陈独秀、李大钊、胡适、鲁迅等人在新文化运动时期的思想与实践——中国近代思想史上的关键阶段。

Why It's Here — and Why It's Hard 为什么放在这里 · 为什么难懂

I love this show. But honestly, I think most international viewers will struggle to feel its full weight. So much of it depends on knowing the historical figures, the political context, and the emotional charge those names carry for Chinese audiences. Without that, it reads as a beautifully shot but distant period drama.

我很喜欢这部剧。但坦白说，我觉得大多数外国观众很难体会到它真正的分量。这部剧高度依赖对中国近代史人物、政治背景和情感联结的了解。缺少这些，它看起来只是一部拍得很美的年代剧。

04

Legend of Lu Zhen

陆贞传奇
Aired播出2013
Genre类别Historical · Romance · Female Lead
Douban豆瓣评分7.4 / 10China's most influential film & TV rating community中国最具影响力的影视评分社区之一
Episodes集数59
Director导演Li Huizhu李慧珠
The Story 剧情简介

After her family falls from grace, a young woman enters the palace as a servant and rises through sheer will, intelligence, and refusal to bend — becoming one of the highest-ranking female officials in the Northern Qi court.

家道中落后，一位年轻女子入宫为婢，凭借意志、智慧与不肯低头，一步步成为北齐宫廷中官位最高的女官之一。

Why It's Here — and Why It's Frustrating 为什么放在这里 · 为什么让人又爱又气

I'll be honest: parts of this show will infuriate you. The romance goes to some genuinely uncomfortable places, and the male lead's decisions are hard to defend. But Lu Zhen herself is one of the best female character arcs I've seen in a Chinese costume drama — she suffers, she schemes, she grows, and she never turns into a saint to please the audience. I'm including it because the female lead is worth it, even if you end up yelling at the screen.

说实话，这部剧有些部分会让你气到不行。感情线有些地方让人非常不适，男主的决定也很难站得住脚。但陆贞这个角色本身，是我在中剧里看过的最好的女性成长弧光之一——她受苦、她算计、她成长，但她从来不会为了讨好观众而变成圣人。我把它放进来，是因为这位女主本身值得一看，即使你看到一半会对着屏幕骂人。
