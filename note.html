A Note to Angie

A Note to Angie

写给 Angie 的话

I know your videos often focus on newer costume dramas, so I'm not sure whether these older shows will be your cup of tea. But I really wanted to share them with you. They may not have today's visual effects, but many of their characters and stories have stayed with me for years.

我知道你的视频更多在介绍近年的古装剧，所以我不确定这些年代更早的作品是不是你的菜。但我真的很想把它们推荐给你。它们不一定有今天的特效和制作质感，却有许多让我一直记得的人物与故事。

Of course, you don't have to love every pick. I just hope one of them catches your interest — and that this little guide helps other Chinese-drama fans discover something new, too.

当然，你不需要喜欢我推荐的每一部。希望其中至少有一部能让你产生兴趣，也希望这份清单能给其他喜欢中国剧的朋友带来一点新的发现。
