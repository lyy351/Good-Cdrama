You Are My Glory

Aired播出2021
Genre类别Modern Romance · Feel-good
Douban豆瓣评分7.1 / 10China's most influential film & TV rating community中国最具影响力的影视评分社区之一
Episodes集数32
Director导演Wang Zhi王之
The Story 剧情简介

A celebrity reconnects with someone from her past, and the two grow closer while pursuing their own ambitions.

一位明星与曾经认识的人重逢，两人在各自的事业与生活中重新靠近。

One of the few modern idol dramas I finished and felt genuinely sweet, comfortable, and un-annoyed by. If you want a lighter love story, start here.

这是我少数看完后觉得甜蜜、舒服、不恼火的现代偶像剧。如果你想看一段相对轻松的爱情故事，可以从它开始。
