Chinese Paladin 3

仙剑奇侠传三
Aired播出2009
Genre类别Fantasy · Ensemble · Adventure
Douban豆瓣评分9.1 / 10China's most influential film & TV rating community中国最具影响力的影视评分社区之一
Episodes集数37
Director导演Li Guoli李国立Known for the Chinese Paladin series, Scarlet Heart
The Story 剧情简介

A fantasy adventure about a group of people whose paths intertwine as they face personal dilemmas and a larger threat.

围绕一群命运交织的人物展开，他们在冒险中面对个人情感、责任与更大的危机。

Why I Recommend It 我为什么推荐

我尤其喜欢它对配角的塑造。故事有明确的主线，但许多配角也拥有自己的情感、选择与成长，不只是围绕主角转动。

Chinese Paladin

仙剑奇侠传一
Aired播出2005
Genre类别Fantasy · Adventure · Tragedy
Douban豆瓣评分9.0 / 10China's most influential film & TV rating community中国最具影响力的影视评分社区之一
Episodes集数34
Director导演Li Guoli李国立Known for the Chinese Paladin series
The Story 剧情简介

A fantasy journey shaped by adventure, fate, and relationships that leave a lasting emotional mark.

一段由冒险、命运与情感牵引的仙侠故事，角色们在旅途中不断面对艰难的选择。

The Proud Twins

小鱼儿与花无缺
Aired播出2005
Genre类别Wuxia · Drama · Villain
Douban豆瓣评分8.0 / 10China's most influential film & TV rating community中国最具影响力的影视评分社区之一
Episodes集数40
Director导演Wong Jing王晶Known for Hong Kong comedy and action classics
The Story 剧情简介

Two young men with very different temperaments find their lives bound together by secrets, rivalries, and the martial-arts world.

小鱼儿与花无缺是一对命运相连、性格迥异的年轻人，他们的故事交织着江湖纷争、身世秘密与复杂的人际关系。

Why I Recommend It 我为什么推荐

Jiang Yuyan has become almost legendary. She's a villain with ambition, ruthlessness, and complex emotions so vivid that fans jokingly call the show "The Legend of Jiang Yuyan."

江玉燕几乎成了这部剧的传奇。她是一个野心、狠心和复杂情感都极具存在感的反派，甚至让观众戏称这部剧为《江玉燕传奇》。

The Swordsman

笑傲江湖（霍建华版）
Aired播出2013
Genre类别Wuxia · Character Study
Douban豆瓣评分6.2 / 10China's most influential film & TV rating community中国最具影响力的影视评分社区之一
Episodes集数42
Director导演Hu Yijuan胡意涓
The Story 剧情简介

A martial-arts drama of rival factions, shifting loyalties, and people caught between power and personal feeling.

江湖门派、权力争斗与个人选择交织在一起，人物的立场和情感不断受到考验。

Mu Guiying Takes Command

穆桂英挂帅
Aired播出2012
Genre类别Historical · War · Female Lead
Douban豆瓣评分7.5 / 10China's most influential film & TV rating community中国最具影响力的影视评分社区之一
Episodes集数40
Director导演Gong Xiaodong宫晓东
The Story 剧情简介

A legendary woman general takes command on the battlefield while navigating duty, politics, family, and a life that remains distinctly her own.

穆桂英是一位拥有真实军事职责与指挥权的女将军，她要在战场、朝廷与个人生活之间做出选择。

Why I Highly Recommend It 我为什么强烈推荐

I love Mu Guiying not because she's tough all the time, but because she's a whole person. She can fight and lead, and she can also love flowers, dress up, and build a family. She doesn't have to become someone else to prove she's a general.

我喜欢穆桂英并不是因为她只表现得强硬，而是因为她是一个完整的人。她能打仗、能指挥，也可以喜欢花、打扮自己、建立家庭。她不需要变成另一个人来证明自己是将军。
